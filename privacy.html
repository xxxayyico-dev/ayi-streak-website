Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026
Last Updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayi Streak ("Ayi Streak", "we", "us", or "our") processes information when you use the Ayi Streak Discord application.

Ayi Streak is a Discord application designed to provide streak tracking, community activity, milestones, points, leaderboards, reminders, and related features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed, why it is processed, how it is used, and how users may request deletion of applicable data.

Important: Ayi Streak only processes information reasonably necessary to provide its Discord features and does not sell Discord user data.

1. Information We Process

Ayi Streak may process information provided by Discord or generated through your use of the application.

2. Discord Account Information

Ayi Streak may process:

  • Discord User ID;
  • Discord username or display name; and
  • Information necessary to identify your membership within a Discord server.

These identifiers allow Ayi Streak to associate streaks and related statistics with the correct Discord user.

3. Discord Server Information

Ayi Streak may process:

  • Discord Server/Guild ID;
  • Server-specific configuration; and
  • Information necessary to operate Ayi Streak within a server.

Ayi Streak uses server-specific data so that activity and statistics remain separated between different Discord servers.

4. Streak and Activity Information

Ayi Streak may store information necessary to operate its streak system, including:

  • Message or activity counts;
  • Current daily streak;
  • Longest streak;
  • Last recorded activity;
  • Streak freezes;
  • Daily points;
  • Daily claim information;
  • Milestone progress; and
  • Other statistics required for Ayi Streak features.

5. Feature and Configuration Information

Ayi Streak may store configuration information required for server features, including administrative settings and other configuration necessary for the bot to operate.

6. Messages, Attachments, and Voice Messages

Ayi Streak may access Discord message information, attachments, images, videos, or voice messages when necessary to determine whether activity qualifies for a streak or to provide a requested feature.

Ayi Streak is designed to use this information for its stated Discord functionality.

Ayi Streak does not intentionally store the actual contents of users' images, videos, or voice messages as part of normal streak profile data.

Where Discord provides message or attachment information necessary for a feature, Ayi Streak processes only information reasonably necessary for that feature.

Ayi Streak does not use Discord message content to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

7. How We Use Information

Ayi Streak uses information only as necessary to provide and maintain its stated functionality.

This may include:

  • Tracking daily activity streaks;
  • Calculating streak lengths;
  • Recording activity counts;
  • Calculating points;
  • Tracking milestones;
  • Assigning milestone-related roles where configured;
  • Maintaining leaderboards;
  • Managing streak freezes;
  • Updating streak-related nicknames where enabled and permitted;
  • Sending reminders or streak-related notifications;
  • Responding to commands;
  • Providing server-specific functionality;
  • Preventing abuse and technical misuse;
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting the application; and
  • Complying with applicable legal or Discord requirements.

Ayi Streak does not use Discord API data for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

8. Server-Specific Data

Ayi Streak is designed to keep user statistics separated by Discord server.

The same Discord user may therefore have different streaks, points, freezes, milestones, or activity statistics in different servers.

A user's statistics in one server are not intentionally used as that user's statistics in another server.

9. How Information Is Shared

Ayi Streak does not sell, rent, or license Discord user data.

Ayi Streak does not provide Discord API data to advertising networks or data brokers.

Information may be processed or disclosed only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide Ayi Streak's functionality;
  • Use service providers necessary to operate the application;
  • Comply with applicable law or legal obligations;
  • Respond to valid legal requests;
  • Protect the security or integrity of Ayi Streak or its users; or
  • Respond to a user's explicit request or direction.

10. Data Retention

Ayi Streak retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality, maintain server statistics, comply with applicable requirements, or address legitimate operational and security needs.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

When information is no longer necessary for Ayi Streak's stated functionality, it may be deleted or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements.

11. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of Ayi Streak.

To request deletion, contact the Ayi Streak development/support team through the official Ayi Streak support channels and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant Discord account and server data.

We may request reasonable information necessary to verify the request and prevent unauthorized deletion requests.

Upon a valid deletion request, applicable personal or API data will be deleted or updated as required, subject to:

  • Applicable legal requirements;
  • Legitimate security requirements;
  • Technical limitations; and
  • Data that we are legally required to retain.

Deleting your Ayi Streak data may cause your streaks, points, milestones, freezes, rankings, and other associated statistics to be permanently lost.

12. Removing Ayi Streak From a Server

A server administrator may remove Ayi Streak from a Discord server at any time.

Removing Ayi Streak from a server does not necessarily mean that all previously stored data is immediately deleted.

If you want your applicable user data deleted, you may submit a deletion request through the official Ayi Streak support channels.

13. Discord

Ayi Streak operates through Discord.

Discord independently processes information according to Discord's own Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and other applicable policies.

Ayi Streak does not control Discord's own processing of information.

Users should review Discord's privacy documentation for information about how Discord processes their information.

14. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information used by Ayi Streak from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Children's Privacy

Ayi Streak does not knowingly target children below the minimum age permitted by Discord or applicable law.

Ayi Streak should not be used in violation of Discord's age requirements or applicable laws.

16. International Data Processing

Because Discord and infrastructure used to operate online services may process information across different countries, information processed through Ayi Streak may be handled in locations outside your country.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Ayi Streak's functionality, data practices, legal requirements, or operational processes change.

The updated Privacy Policy will be published at the publicly available Privacy Policy URL.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this document indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

18. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions, concerns, or data deletion requests, contact the Ayi Streak development/support team through the official Ayi Streak support channels.

When submitting a request, please clearly state that the request concerns Ayi Streak and specify the Discord account or server information relevant to the request.
